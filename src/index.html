About

Morten Haraldsen

Software developer and architect. My work ranges from mapping business processes to low-level software and data optimisation — for the last 17 years as a principal engineer, where I started as employee number one.

Away from the keyboard: cameras, the workshop, and the occasional sensor wired into the house.

ethlo used to be my company name and has stayed with me ever since. It comes from the Linux ifconfig output, which usually lists eth and lo as the standard network interfaces. Short and sweet, so I sign the things I make with it.
